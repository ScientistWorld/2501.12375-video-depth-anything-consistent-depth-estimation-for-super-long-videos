# Research Direction

Future work should stay within monocular long-video depth estimation. Acceptable improvements include better temporal consistency mechanisms, more robust long-video stitching, improved video depth heads, or stronger training strategies that still solve the same RGB-video-to-depth task.

Out-of-scope changes include replacing the task with multi-view reconstruction requiring camera poses, using paid external APIs, evaluating on a different dataset without also reporting the fixed benchmark, or gaining performance by directly fitting the evaluation ground truth.
